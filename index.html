04 · Now · Co-Founder & CMO, Matech

Matech work

Two products from Matech's payment platform: themes for the CerePay terminal, and the Cerebro console that runs the fleet.

Base theme · CerePay POS + Cerebro TMS

Matech Default

Matech's own theme for the CerePay terminal and the Cerebro console. Every palette starts here and follows the same three rules.

Lock the product

Layout, copy and motion stay the same as Matech Default in every theme. Staff learn one terminal and one console, not nine.

Swap the tokens

Each theme brings its own palette, typeface, corner shape and chrome edge: the parts people actually notice.

Keep it readable

Text holds 4.5:1 or better against its fill. Display type may drop to 3:1.
